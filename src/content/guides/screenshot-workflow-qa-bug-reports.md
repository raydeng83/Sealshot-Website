A bug report is a screenshot plus everything the screenshot does not say. Most
of the back-and-forth on a ticket is a developer reconstructing context the
reporter had on screen and did not capture.

This guide will cover what to capture (and what to record instead), annotation
that survives review, stripping customer data before the image reaches a public
tracker, and keeping evidence organised across a testing session.

See [Explain](/docs/workflows/explain/) for the annotate-and-attach loop, and
[the editor](/docs/guide/editor/) for non-destructive markup.
