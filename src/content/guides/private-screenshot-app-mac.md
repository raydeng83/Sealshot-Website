A screenshot library is an unusually complete record: the messages, the
dashboards, the half-finished documents, the things you captured precisely
because they mattered. It deserves more scrutiny than most folders on your Mac.

This guide will cover the questions worth asking — where captures are stored,
what leaves the machine and when, what an account requirement implies, what
telemetry collects — and how encryption at rest changes the answer if the laptop
is lost.

Sealshot stores everything locally, can encrypt its library and unlock with
Touch ID, and has no account and no telemetry. See
[Security](/docs/guide/security/) and our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
