This guide will cover what macOS gives you out of the box, where Spotlight stops
being enough, and how a library that runs OCR on every capture turns "somewhere
in the last three months" into one search box.

Sealshot indexes the text in every capture on your Mac, and nowhere else. See
[the Library](/docs/guide/library/) for how that search works today.
