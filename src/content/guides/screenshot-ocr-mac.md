Text in a screenshot is just pixels until something reads it. macOS ships Live
Text, which handles the one-off case well and the *every* case not at all —
because you have to remember to invoke it, on the right image, one image at a
time.

This guide will cover Live Text and its limits, when OCR accuracy drops and what
to do about it, and what changes when recognition runs automatically at capture
time and feeds a searchable index.

Sealshot runs OCR on device — see [AI features](/docs/guide/ai/) and
[Extract](/docs/workflows/extract/).
