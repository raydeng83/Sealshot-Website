macOS `⌘⇧4` captures what is on screen. It has no answer for the page that is
four screens tall, which is why "scrolling screenshot" is one of the most-asked
Mac capture questions.

This guide will cover the browser's own full-page capture, stitching approaches
and where they visibly seam, and when a plain sequence of captures in one
library beats a single tall image.

Start with [Capture](/docs/guide/capture/) for what Sealshot captures today.
