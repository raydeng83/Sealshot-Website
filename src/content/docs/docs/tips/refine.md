Half of this stage is work you do. The other half already happened while
the capture was landing.

## Annotation that stays consistent

- **Set a tool's style once.** Each
  [annotation tool](/docs/guide/editor/#annotation-tools) remembers its own
  colour, stroke, and opacity — your next arrow looks like your last, across
  captures and across days.
- **Keep marks readable anywhere.** Add an **Outline** colour so arrows and
  text stay visible over both light and dark screenshots. It has its own
  width control, so a thin casing on a busy screenshot is possible.
- **Two kinds of arrow.** **Line Arrow** is straight, **Free Arrow** follows
  your hand — and both take adjustable end-caps plus solid, dashed, or
  dotted styling.
- **Style text down to the word.** The text tool carries a full font picker,
  a weight slider, and **per-word colour and size** — enough to highlight
  one term in a sentence without adding a second annotation.
- **Numbered badges count for you.** Click in the order you want people to
  read; the numbers auto-increment.

## Making marks land on busy screens

- **Drop shadows have a position pad.** Toggle a shadow on, then aim it with
  the pad or one of eight direction presets — the cheapest way to lift a
  mark off a cluttered background.
- **Annotations are layers.** Send them forward or backward from the
  properties panel when a label ends up under an arrow.
- **⌘-scroll zooms toward your cursor** — from
  [any tool](/docs/guide/editor/#zoom-and-navigation), with no trip to the
  zoom control.

## Nothing you do here is permanent

- **Undo survives everything.** ⌘Z reaches back across quits, capture
  switches, even deletes and metadata edits — one
  [global timeline](/docs/guide/editor/#undo-that-survives-relaunch).
- **Duplicate before you experiment.** Copy a whole capture to try a
  different crop or annotation set with the original untouched.
- **Live Capture scenes are fully reversible.** **Auto Arrange** tidies two
  or more windows into a clean, non-overlapping layout in one step, and
  **Revert to Original Image** puts every window back where it was
  captured — see [Live Capture
  scenes](/docs/guide/editor/#live-capture-scenes).

## Cleanup you may not know is there

- **Enhance Clarity and Remove Background.** Sharpen a low-resolution grab,
  or lift a subject off its background — both under [Enhance Clarity and
  Remove Background](/docs/guide/editor/#enhance-clarity-and-remove-background).
- **Insert images.** Drop a logo, a callout, or a second screenshot onto the
  canvas rather than compositing somewhere else.
- **Crop stays adjustable.** [Crop and
  resize](/docs/guide/editor/#crop-and-resize) is an annotation like any
  other, so trimming early doesn't cost you the pixels.

## The work your Mac already did

- **The title and summary are already written.** Every capture arrives with
  a generated [title, summary, and Smart
  Keywords](/docs/guide/ai/#automatic-titles-summaries-and-smart-keywords).
  The summary is yours to edit — and there's a **Revert to Generated
  Summary** if your edit turns out worse.
- **Turn a table screenshot into data.** [Extract Structured
  Data](/docs/guide/ai/#extract-structured-data) pulls tables, line items,
  totals, and vendor fields out as copyable text — and caches the result in
  the `.seal`, so asking again is instant.
- **Select text straight off the image.** [OCR Live
  Text](/docs/guide/editor/#ocr-live-text) works on any capture, which beats
  retyping an error message to search for it.
- **Let it scan for secrets automatically.** Turn on auto-scan in
  [Settings → On-Device AI](/docs/guide/redaction/#running-a-scan) and every
  capture is checked the moment it opens, before you have a chance to forget.
- **Solid fill, not blur, for real secrets.** Blur can hint at short
  strings; [solid fill](/docs/guide/editor/#blur-and-redaction) is
  unrecoverable in the export.
