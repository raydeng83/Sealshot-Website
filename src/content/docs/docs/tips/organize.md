---
title: "Tips: Organize"
description: Search that reads inside images, filters, collections, and the safety net under deletes.
---

You can manage and organize the captured items in library efficiently. Sealshot indexes 
every capture on arrival, so most organizing takes care of
itself. These tips cover finding things fast and cleaning up safely.

## Search bar

- **Search inside your captures.**
  [Search](/docs/guide/library/#search) reads the *text in the image* — an
  error message, an invoice number, a vendor name — not just filenames.
- **Generated Keywords.** Captures automatically generate keywords, which can
  be used later for searching.
- **Your tags stay yours.** You can add tags to each capture. The [tags you
  add](/docs/guide/library/#titles-tags-and-the-info-panel) are used for search
  as well.

## Filters and preview

- **Space bar for an instant preview.**
  [Quick Look](/docs/guide/library/#quick-look) any capture without opening
  the editor; ⌘-scroll resizes the preview.
- **Filter by tag, date, or media type.** The
  [sidebar filters](/docs/guide/library/#sections-and-filters) narrow by tag
  or by year and month, and the media filter shows images only or videos
  only.
- **Filter bar.** The
  filter bar near center top of library shows the current filters applied.
  You can remove or clear the filters easily from filter bar.


## Collections and favorites

- **Favorites folder.** You can add captured items to Favorites folder
  [collections](/docs/guide/library/#collections-and-favorites).
- **Create collections.** You can create your own collections and organize captures
  based on your preferred folders.


## Trash bin

- **Trash grace period.** Trashed captures purge after
  a number of days you set in **Settings → General → Trash** (7 by default),
  or empty it yourself from the Trash header.
- **Delete Forever.** Delete forever can't be recovered.
