**Released July 16, 2026** ·
[Download](https://github.com/raydeng83/Sealshot-Release/releases/tag/v0.7.1)

A polish release across the editor, recording, and security — led by one
app-wide undo timeline.

## What's new since 0.7.0

### Editor

- **One undo timeline** — images, videos, metadata edits, and selection are
  all undoable steps, and each undo names what it undid. See
  [Undo that survives relaunch](/docs/guide/editor/#undo-that-survives-relaunch).
- **Free Arrow** — draw an arrow along any path, alongside the straight
  **Line Arrow**.
- **Outline colors** — pen, line, arrow, shape, step, and text annotations
  can carry a casing color with its own width control, so marks stay
  readable on any background.
- **Text tool overhaul** — weight slider, per-tool color and opacity memory,
  a visible text-box frame with resize dots, and typography remembered
  across launches.
- **Color-faithful export** — captures render and export without lightened
  text.
- **Duplicate a capture** from the editor, the recent strip, or the Library.
- Dragging a capture with a [focus area](/docs/guide/editor/#focus-area) to
  Finder exports just that area.
- [Extract Structured Data](/docs/guide/ai/#extract-structured-data) results
  appear in one consolidated view, exports default to the image's name,
  cancelling takes effect immediately, and extraction uses the enhanced
  on-device model when it's downloaded.
- The toolbar and tabs freeze during blocking AI actions; navigating away
  cancels the action.
- Fit Width / Fit Height no longer leave a stray scrollbar, and a remembered
  video reopens with its player panel in place.

### Capture & OCR

- **OCR recognizes non-Latin scripts**, with automatic language detection.
- **Smart Redaction catches more** — 2FA backup-code grids, role names,
  ticket IDs, masked card numbers, and street addresses. See
  [Smart Redaction](/docs/guide/redaction/).
- A cancelled capture no longer raises the editor over the app you were
  using.

### Recording

- The [recording HUD](/docs/guide/recording/#while-recording) shows a
  Recording/Paused label beside its status icon.
- The record prompt appears over full-screen apps, on the display being
  recorded.
- System and microphone audio are recorded at native 48 kHz.
- Each recording shortcut stops only its own recording mode, and a finished
  recording is selected automatically in the recent strip.

### Library

- Resize the [Quick Look](/docs/guide/library/#preview) preview with the
  −/+ buttons, and grid tiles with ⌘-scroll.
- Play a video straight from its play badge in the strip; clicking the
  thumbnail opens it paused.
- Drag multiple captures to Finder with a progress bar and Cancel.
- **Empty Trash** clears the Trash in one click, and multi-select stays fast
  in large libraries.

### Security

- **Idle auto-lock follows system-wide idle time**, not just activity inside
  the app.
- Menus and keyboard shortcuts are gated while the library is locked.
- Turning encryption on or off shows reliable progress and no longer damages
  video packages.

### Other

- Deleted items are kept **7 days by default** before being purged, and the
  retention setting applies reliably to every trashed item.
- Your own tags are preserved when Smart Keywords are regenerated.
